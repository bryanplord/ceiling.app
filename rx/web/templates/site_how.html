How it works.

Four steps. You do one of them once.

1

Connect your watch

Link your Garmin account once. Ceiling reads your workouts, heart rate, sleep and HRV from then on, and your history from the last year.

2

Wake up to your prescription

Before you're out of bed, last night is read against your normal: sleep, HRV, resting heart rate, and what you've done lately.

You get one call: day off, easy, steady or hard. Then the session, the heart rate to hold, the least that still counts, the most you can do, and what tomorrow looks like.

3

Train

Ride, run or ski. The prescription gives you options for the activities you actually do, with the same heart rate whatever you pick.

Afterwards, the session is graded against what was prescribed: did you stay in range, and did you go too long.

4

It adapts

Tomorrow is re-planned from what you actually did, how you slept and anything you logged: a few drinks, a scratchy throat, a long day.

Over weeks, Ceiling learns what those do to you, and tells you.
